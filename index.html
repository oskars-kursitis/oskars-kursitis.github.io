Your round, sorted.

GigPro is a phone app for self-employed people who run a round — gardeners, window cleaners, cleaners, dog walkers. Plan the day, send the invoice when the job's done, and give jobs to your crew.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

Plan the day

Put the jobs in order — GigPro works out the drive times, when you'll get to each customer and when you'll be home.

Job done → invoice

Tick a job off, send the invoice by text, WhatsApp or email. If you use Xero, a copy goes into your books.

Give jobs to your crew

Swipe a job and it's on their phone. See their progress as the day goes.

Bring your round in

Get your customers in from Xero, and the jobs you've already booked from your calendar — no retyping.

How GigPro uses your Google data

If you choose Connect Google, GigPro asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It uses it for one thing: finding the jobs you've already booked, and which customer each one is for, so you don't have to type your round in again.

  • Events and tasks go straight from Google to your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else.
  • GigPro never changes, adds or deletes anything in your Google account.
  • Your Google data isn't used for ads, isn't sold, and isn't used to train AI.
  • Disconnect any time in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

GigPro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our privacy policy.
